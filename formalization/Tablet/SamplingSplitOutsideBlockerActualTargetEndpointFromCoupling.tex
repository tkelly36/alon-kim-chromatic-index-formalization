\begin{helper}
Fix an actual paired mixed surface
\(\widehat S_j(\omega),\widehat C_\omega,\lambda,M_j(\omega)\) from
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerPairedMixedMeasureSurface}\), and fix
the marginal-preserving coupling data
\(\mathcal C\) from
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerMarginalPreservingCouplingData}\) on
the same atom family.  Assume that the surface's paired measure and paired
cells are exactly the paired measure and paired cells of \(\mathcal C\).
Assume the local replacement hypotheses for \(\phi\) and \(\beta\): \(\phi\)
is injective, adjacencies inside \(X\cup\{r\}\) are preserved, every private
vertex \(\beta(x,z)\) is adjacent to its intended \(\phi(x)\), no private
vertex for \(x\) is adjacent to a wrong embedded candidate \(\phi(y)\) with
\(y\in X\), no private vertex is adjacent to \(\phi(r)\), and every neighbor
of \(\phi(x)\) in \(G'\) is either an embedded neighbor from
\(\phi(X\cup\{r\})\) or the private replacement of a source outside neighbor
of \(x\).  Assume also that the actual list \(z_0,\ldots,z_{n-1}\) covers
every source outside blocker adjacent to \(X\), that the target law \(\nu'\)
satisfies the Bernoulli activation atom formula and priority lower-rectangle
law with a parameter \(p\in[0,1]\), and that the target endpoint event
\[
  E'_{\phi(X)}
  =\{(A',\pi'): I_{G'}(A',\pi')\cap\phi(X)\ne\emptyset\}
\]
is measurable.  Then for every atom \(\omega\),
\[
  \operatorname{ofReal}(w(\omega)M_n(\omega))
  =
  \nu'(E'_{\phi(X)}\cap C'_\omega).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(\omega\).  The measured-stage field of
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerPairedMixedMeasureSurface}\), applied
with \(j=n\), gives
\[
  \operatorname{ofReal}(w(\omega)M_n(\omega))
  =
  \lambda(\widehat S_n(\omega)\cap\widehat C_\omega).
\]
We first compare \(\widehat S_n(\omega)\), on \(\widehat C_\omega\), with the
second-coordinate cylinder over the displayed target local/private endpoint
event.  Let \(D'_{\phi(X)}\) be the event that there is \(x\in X\) such that
\(\phi(x)\) is active, \(\phi(x)\) beats every active embedded neighbor
\(\phi(y)\) with \(y\in X\cup\{r\}\), and each listed private blocker
\(\beta(x,z_i)\) with \(x\in B_i\) fails the opposite strict test
\[
  \neg(\beta(x,z_i)\in A',\
       \pi'(\phi(x))<\pi'(\beta(x,z_i)),\
       0\le\pi'(\beta(x,z_i))\le1).
\]
This exact displayed event is measurable by
\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerTargetDisplayedEndpointMeasurable},
applied to \(G,\phi,r,X,z_i,B_i,\beta\). Its statement uses the same
candidate, embedded-neighbor comparisons, and private blocker tests,
including the unit-interval clause. This is independent of the assumed
measurability of the true endpoint event. The endpoint boundary lemma will
later compare these two events in measure.

Let \(\zeta=((A,\pi),(A',\pi'))\in\widehat C_\omega\).  Expanding the
mixed-stage definition in
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerPairedMixedMeasureSurface}\) at cut
\(n\), there are no retained source blockers.  Membership in
\(\widehat S_n(\omega)\) asks for \(x\in X\) such that \(\phi(x)\in A'\), the
embedded priority tests against \(\phi(X\cup\{r\})\) hold, and every already
replaced outside blocker \(z_i\) with \(x\in B_i\) fails through the private
target blocker \(\beta(x,z_i)\).  The source-side activation and embedded
comparison clauses are redundant on \(\widehat C_\omega\), because the paired
cell synchronizes the common activation trace and priorities on the source
coordinates containing \(X\cup\{r\}\).  Thus, for paired states in
\(\widehat C_\omega\),
\[
  \zeta\in\widehat S_n(\omega)
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \zeta_2\in D'_{\phi(X)} .
\]
Consequently
\[
  \widehat S_n(\omega)\cap\widehat C_\omega
  =
  \{\zeta:\zeta_2\in D'_{\phi(X)}\}\cap\widehat C_\omega .
\]

Because the surface and \(\mathcal C\) use the same paired measure and paired
cells, the target restricted marginal law from
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerMarginalPreservingCouplingData}\),
applied to the displayed measurable event \(D'_{\phi(X)}\), gives
\[
  \lambda(\{\zeta:\zeta_2\in D'_{\phi(X)}\}\cap\widehat C_\omega)
  =
  \nu'(D'_{\phi(X)}\cap C'_\omega).
\]

Now apply
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerEndpointBoundaryCellReduction}\) on the
target graph.  Use candidate type \(\{x:V:x\in X\}\), candidate set the full
finite subtype, embedding \(x\mapsto\phi(x)\), local coordinate set
\(\phi(X\cup\{r\})\), blocker \(b_i(x)=\beta(x,z_i)\), and blocker predicate
\(H_i(x)\) equal to \(x\in B_i\).  The local-self condition is
\(\phi(x)\in\phi(X\cup\{r\})\).  If \(H_i(x)\), the surface's
candidate-set identity gives \(Gxz_i\), while \(hOutside_i\) gives
\(z_i\notin X\cup\{r\}\); hence the intended-adjacency hypothesis for
\(\beta\) proves that \(b_i(x)\) is adjacent to \(\phi(x)\).

For the target neighbor-cover condition, let \(w'\) be adjacent to
\(\phi(x)\).  By the target-neighbor exhaustion hypothesis, either
\(w'=\phi(y)\) for some \(y\in X\cup\{r\}\), in which case \(w'\) is local,
or \(w'=\beta(x,z)\) for a source outside neighbor
\(z\notin X\cup\{r\}\) of \(x\).  In the second case the actual
outside-blocker coverage gives \(k\) with \(z_k=z\).  The candidate-set
identity then gives \(x\in B_k\), and substituting \(z=z_k\), using proof
irrelevance for the subtype proof in the argument of \(\beta\), rewrites
\(w'\) as the listed blocker \(b_k(x)\).  The wrong-candidate and non-root
hypotheses ensure that no private vertex attached to another candidate, and
no private vertex at the copied root, supplies an additional endpoint
neighbor outside this local-plus-listed classification.

Therefore the boundary-reduction node, with the target activation atom and
priority rectangle laws and the cell \(C'_\omega\), gives the mass identity
\[
  \nu'(E'_{\phi(X)}\cap C'_\omega)
  =
  \nu'(D'_{\phi(X)}\cap C'_\omega).
\]
Combining this equality with the measured-stage identity at \(j=n\) and the
target restricted marginal identity proves the target endpoint mass identity
for the fixed \(\omega\).  Since \(\omega\) was arbitrary, the identity holds
on every atom.
\end{proof}
