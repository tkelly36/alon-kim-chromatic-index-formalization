\begin{lemma}
Let \(\nu\) and \(\nu'\) be two activation-priority product laws with the
same activation parameter \(p\).  On the source side, let \(C\) be the
already fixed coordinate set and let \(D\) be a disjoint finite extension
set; prescribe a base activation pattern on \(C\), an extension activation
pattern on \(D\), lower and upper priority cuts on \(C\), and lower cuts on
\(D\).  Make the analogous choices \(C',D'\) for the target side.  Assume
that the two base cells have the same normalized real mass, that the finite
activation factors on \(D\) and \(D'\) are equal, and that the finite
priority-cut products on the extension cuts are equal.

Then the two one-sided extended cylinder masses are represented by
nonnegative real numbers \(m^-\) and \(m^+\), and these real numbers are
equal.  More precisely, each side is obtained by applying the finite
coordinate-extension mass formula to its own measure, and the resulting
source and target extension weights satisfy \(m^-=m^+\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \(\noderef{SamplingFiniteCoordinateCellExtensionMass}\) first to the
source law \(\nu\), using the source old coordinates \(C\), source extension
coordinates \(D\), the source base and extension activation patterns, and the
source lower and upper priority cuts.  Its hypotheses are exactly the
one-sided product-law hypotheses: \(0\le p\le1\), \(C\cap D=\emptyset\), the
base activation and priority cuts lie on \(C\), the extension activation and
extension lower cuts lie on \(D\), the relevant cut sets are disjoint, all
thresholds lie in \([0,1]\), and \(\nu\) satisfies the activation-atom and
priority lower-rectangle laws.  The conclusion gives nonnegative real
numbers \(b^-\) and \(m^-\) such that \(b^-\) is the mass of the source base
cell, \(m^-\) is the mass of the source extended cylinder, and
\[
m^- =
b^-\,p^{|A^-_D|}(1-p)^{|D^-|-|A^-_D|}
  \prod_{v\in K^-} t^-_v .
\]

Apply the same node to the target law \(\nu'\) with \(C',D'\) and the target
activation and priority data.  This yields nonnegative real numbers \(b^+\)
and \(m^+\), identifies \(b^+\) with the target base-cell mass, identifies
\(m^+\) with the target extended-cylinder mass, and gives
\[
m^+ =
b^+\,p^{|A^+_{D'}|}(1-p)^{|D'|-|A^+_{D'}|}
  \prod_{v'\in K^+} t^+_{v'} .
\]

The base-mass compatibility hypothesis applies to the two displayed
base-mass identities just obtained, so \(b^-=b^+\).  After replacing \(b^+\)
by \(b^-\), the remaining hypotheses say that the two activation factors are
equal and that the two priority products are equal.  Therefore
\(\noderef{SamplingFiniteProductMatchedCoordinateExtensionTransport}\)
applies to the two extension formulas and gives \(m^-=m^+\).  The
nonnegativity and the two measure-identification clauses are precisely the
ones returned by the two one-sided applications of
\(\noderef{SamplingFiniteCoordinateCellExtensionMass}\), so the asserted
matched extension-mass package follows.
\end{proof}
