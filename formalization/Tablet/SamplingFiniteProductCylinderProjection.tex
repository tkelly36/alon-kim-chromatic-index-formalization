\begin{lemma}
Let \(S\) and \(T\) be measurable spaces and let \(\nu\) and \(\nu'\) be
\(s\)-finite probability measures on them.  For every source event
\(E\subseteq S\) and target event \(F\subseteq T\), the
product law satisfies the two cylinder projection identities
\[
(\nu\otimes\nu')(E\times T)=\nu(E),\qquad
(\nu\otimes\nu')(S\times F)=\nu'(F).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The product-measure rectangle formula gives
\[
(\nu\otimes\nu')(E\times T)=\nu(E)\nu'(T)
\]
and
\[
(\nu\otimes\nu')(S\times F)=\nu(S)\nu'(F).
\]
The hypotheses say \(\nu(S)=1\) and \(\nu'(T)=1\).  Substituting these two
normalizations into the displayed rectangle formulas gives
\((\nu\otimes\nu')(E\times T)=\nu(E)\cdot 1=\nu(E)\) and
\((\nu\otimes\nu')(S\times F)=1\cdot\nu'(F)=\nu'(F)\), as required.
\end{proof}
