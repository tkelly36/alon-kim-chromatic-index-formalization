\begin{lemma}
Let $H,H_c$ be finite hypergraphs on the same vertex type, with edge-index
sets $E,F$, and let $C\subseteq E$. Suppose a map $i:F\to E$ satisfies
$H_c(f)=H(i(f))$ and $e\notin C$ if and only if $e=i(f)$ for some $f$.
On $R=\{e\in E:e\notin C\}$ define $H_R(e)=H(e)$.
Then there is an injective map $j:R\to F$ with $i(j(e))=e$.
Moreover $H_R$ is a subhypergraph of both $H_c$ and $H$, and
$\deg_{H_R}(v)\le\deg_{H_c}(v)$ for every vertex $v$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each $e\in R$, choose a preimage $j(e)$ using the coverage hypothesis.
If $j(e)=j(f)$ then applying $i$ gives $e=f$, proving injectivity.
The edge identity gives $H_c(j(e))=H(i(j(e)))=H(e)=H_R(e)$.
Thus $j$ realizes \noderef{SubhypergraphOf} into $H_c$; the subtype
inclusion realizes it into $H$. The degree assertion follows from the
degree clause of \noderef{SubhypergraphInheritance}.
\end{proof}
