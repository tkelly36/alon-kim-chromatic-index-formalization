\begin{definition}
For a hypergraph $\mathcal F$, a vertex $v$, and a natural number $k$,
let $\operatorname{FreshEdge}_k(\mathcal F,v)$ be the hypergraph obtained
from $\mathcal F$ by adjoining $k-1$ fresh vertices and one new edge
consisting of $v$ together with all of those fresh vertices.  The old edge
labels are retained, and one new edge label is added for the fresh edge.
\end{definition}
