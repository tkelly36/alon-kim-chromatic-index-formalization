\begin{helper}
Let $G$ be a $\Delta$-regular finite graph, let $r$ be a vertex, and let
$X\subseteq N_G(r)$.  If $\mathcal J_2$ is the family of independent
two-element subsets of $X$, then
\[
|\mathcal J_2|\le \frac{\Delta^2}{2}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Every member of $\mathcal J_2$ is, in particular, a two-element subset of
$X$.  Hence
\[
|\mathcal J_2|\le \binom{|X|}{2}.
\]
Because $X\subseteq N_G(r)$ and $G$ is $\Delta$-regular, we have
$|X|\le |N_G(r)|=\Delta$.  Therefore
\[
\binom{|X|}{2}
=\frac{|X|(|X|-1)}2
\le \frac{|X|^2}{2}
\le \frac{\Delta^2}{2}.
\]
Combining the two displayed inequalities gives
$|\mathcal J_2|\le \Delta^2/2$, as required.
\end{proof}
