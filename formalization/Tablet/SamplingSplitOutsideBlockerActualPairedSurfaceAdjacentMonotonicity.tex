\begin{helper}
Let \(M_j(\omega)\) be the mixed mass family on the actual paired mixed
surface.  Assume that for each \(k<n\) and atom \(\omega\) the two
consecutive stages are decomposed on the same paired noncurrent context:
there are real numbers \(U_{k,\omega}\), \(P^-_{k,\omega}\), and
\(P^+_{k,\omega}\) such that
\[
M_k(\omega)=U_{k,\omega}+P^-_{k,\omega},\qquad
M_{k+1}(\omega)=U_{k,\omega}+P^+_{k,\omega},
\]
and the actual one-step product comparison gives
\[
  P^-_{k,\omega}\le P^+_{k,\omega}.
\]
Then every adjacent replacement step is monotone:
\[
  M_k(\omega)\le M_{k+1}(\omega)\qquad(k<n).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(k<n\) and \(\omega\).  By the shared-context comparison hypothesis,
choose \(U_{k,\omega}\), \(P^-_{k,\omega}\), and \(P^+_{k,\omega}\) with
\[
M_k(\omega)=U_{k,\omega}+P^-_{k,\omega},\qquad
M_{k+1}(\omega)=U_{k,\omega}+P^+_{k,\omega},
\]
and \(P^-_{k,\omega}\le P^+_{k,\omega}\).  Substituting these two displayed
equalities reduces the desired inequality to
\[
  U_{k,\omega}+P^-_{k,\omega}
  \le U_{k,\omega}+P^+_{k,\omega},
\]
which follows by adding the same unchanged term \(U_{k,\omega}\) to both
sides of the one-step inequality.

This is the algebraic endpoint of the paired-surface comparison.  The
substantial lower input is the shared-context decomposition itself: it must
be produced before projection, with all noncurrent retained-source tests and
already-private target tests kept inside the same paired event, and with the
current boundary inequality supplied by the registered fixed-stage/product
comparison such as
\(\noderef{SamplingSplitOutsideBlockerActualOneStepMixedProductComparison}\).
The present node only cancels the common paired contribution; it does not
compare separated intermediate residuals.
\end{proof}
