\begin{helper}
Let \(\gamma>0\), and let \(S_x,S_y,S_z\) be finite sets.  Then
\[
\int_0^1\int_0^z\int_0^y
x^{|S_x|}y^{|S_y|}z^{|S_z|}\,dx\,dy\,dz
=
\frac1{\gamma^3}\int_0^\gamma\int_z^\gamma\int_y^\gamma
\left(1-\frac{x}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_x|}
\left(1-\frac{y}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_y|}
\left(1-\frac{z}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_z|}\,dx\,dy\,dz .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Apply the affine change of variables
\[
x'=\gamma(1-x),\qquad y'=\gamma(1-y),\qquad z'=\gamma(1-z)
\]
to the region \(0\le x\le y\le z\le1\).  Since \(\gamma>0\), this is a
bijective \(C^1\) change of variables from that simplex onto
\(0\le z'\le y'\le x'\le\gamma\).  Its Jacobian determinant has absolute value
\(\gamma^3\), or equivalently \(dx\,dy\,dz=\gamma^{-3}\,dx'\,dy'\,dz'\) after
moving the factor to the right-hand integral.  The coordinate powers transform
as
\[
x^{|S_x|}=\left(1-\frac{x'}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_x|},\quad
y^{|S_y|}=\left(1-\frac{y'}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_y|},\quad
z^{|S_z|}=\left(1-\frac{z'}{\gamma}\right)^{|S_z|}.
\]
Rewriting the transformed domain in the order
\(0\le z'\le y'\le x'\le\gamma\) gives exactly the right-hand iterated
integral, with the factor \(\gamma^{-3}=1/\gamma^3\).  Renaming
\((x',y',z')\) back to \((x,y,z)\) yields the displayed identity.
\end{proof}
