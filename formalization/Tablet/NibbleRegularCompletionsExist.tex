\begin{lemma}
If $H$ has finite edge-index set, is $k$-uniform, and has maximum degree
at most $D$, then for any retained lists $M$ there exists a nibble
completion datum for $H,M,\Delta=kD$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{UniformHypergraphLineDegreeBound} the line graph has degree
at most $kD$. For each color $a$, restrict it to the finite subtype
$\{e:a\in M(e)\}$. Its neighbor set injects into the original neighbor
set, so its degrees are also at most $kD$. Apply
\noderef{FiniteSimpleRegularCompletion} to this graph, obtaining a finite
$kD$-regular graph and an injective induced embedding. Choose a bijection
from the completion vertex type to $\operatorname{Fin}(n_a)$, where $n_a$
is its cardinality, and transport adjacency and the embedding along it.
Bijections preserve neighbor cardinalities and adjacency, so the transported
graph is regular and the embedding is induced and injective. Choice over
the colors supplies these objects simultaneously. They are exactly the
fields of \noderef{NibbleCompletionData}; the construction also applies
to an empty color class.
\end{proof}
