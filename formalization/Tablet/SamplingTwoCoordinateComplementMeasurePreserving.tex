\begin{helper}
Let \(V\) be finite and let \(u\ne v\) be two distinguished coordinates.
The map
\[
q\longmapsto \bigl(q(u),q(v),(q(w))_{w\in V\setminus\{u,v\}}\bigr)
\]
from \(\mathbb R^V\) to
\(\mathbb R\times\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^{V\setminus\{u,v\}}\) preserves
Lebesgue product measure.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Choose the index equivalence which lists the two distinguished coordinates
first and then lists the complementary subtype
\(W=\{w\in V:w\ne u,\ w\ne v\}\).  The displayed map is the composition of
this coordinate permutation with the standard finite-product splitting
\(\mathbb R^{\{0,1\}\sqcup W}\cong\mathbb R^{\{0,1\}}\times\mathbb R^W\),
the standard identification \(\mathbb R^{\{0,1\}}\cong\mathbb R\times
\mathbb R\), and product associativity.  Each of these maps is one of the
standard measurable equivalences for finite products and preserves product
Lebesgue measure.  Their composition therefore preserves product Lebesgue
measure, and it is exactly the displayed coordinate-splitting map.
\end{proof}
