\begin{lemma}
For every finite simple graph $G$, vertex $v$, and natural number $M$,
$b^{\mathbb R}_{G,M}(v)=b_{G,M}(v)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Substitute the real cast of $M$ into \noderef{LocalBParameterReal}.
The three terms are exactly those in \noderef{LocalBParameter}, including
when $M=0$. In particular this applies to the natural number $kn$, whose
real cast is the product of the real casts of $k$ and $n$.
\end{proof}
