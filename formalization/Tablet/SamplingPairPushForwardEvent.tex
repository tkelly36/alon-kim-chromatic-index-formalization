\begin{helper}
Let $\mu$ be the push-forward law of the activation--priority output set in
\noderef{RandomIndependentSetSampling}, and let $\nu$ be the underlying law
on pairs $(A,\pi)$.  For any two vertices $u,v$,
\[
\operatorname{ofReal}\!\left(\sum_S
  {\bf 1}_{\{u,v\}\subseteq S}\,\mu(S)\right)
=
\nu\!\left(\{(A,\pi):\{u,v\}\subseteq I(A,\pi)\}\right),
\]
where
\[
I(A,\pi)=\{z\in A:\pi(w)<\pi(z)\text{ for every }w\in A\cap N_G(z)\}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{SamplingFiniteSetPushForwardEvent} with $Q=\{u,v\}$.
Its hypotheses are exactly the nonnegativity and push-forward identities
assumed here. Its measurable disjoint output-fiber partition and finite-sum
identity give the assertion. No distinctness is required: when $u=v$,
the finite set $\{u,v\}$ is the singleton $\{u\}$, and the same argument applies.
\end{proof}
