\begin{helper}
Let \(V\) be finite, let \(u\ne v\), and let
\(B_U,B_T\subseteq V\setminus\{u,v\}\) be disjoint.  If
\(0\le b\le a\le1\), then in the complementary coordinate space
\(\mathbb R^{V\setminus\{u,v\}}\) the set of all complementary priority
vectors \(r\) satisfying \(r(w)\in[0,1]\) for every complementary coordinate,
\(r(z)<a\) for \(z\in B_U\), and \(r(z)<b\) for \(z\in B_T\), has volume
\[
a^{|B_U|}b^{|B_T|}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let \(W=V\setminus\{u,v\}\), and define
\[
U(w)=
\begin{cases}
a,&w\in B_U,\\
b,&w\in B_T,\\
1,&w\notin B_U\cup B_T.
\end{cases}
\]
The half-open product box \(\prod_{w\in W}[0,U(w))\) is contained in the
mixed chamber section: on \(B_U\) and \(B_T\) it gives the required strict
upper inequalities, while on all other coordinates it lies in \([0,1]\).
By \noderef{SamplingTwoCoordinateFiberBoxVolume}, this half-open box has
volume \(a^{|B_U|}b^{|B_T|}\).

The mixed chamber section is contained in the closed product box
\(\prod_{w\in W}[0,U(w)]\).  The standard finite product formula for closed
boxes gives this closed box the same volume
\(\prod_{w\in W}U(w)\).  The same cardinality computation as in
\noderef{SamplingTwoCoordinateFiberBoxVolume} identifies that product with
\(a^{|B_U|}b^{|B_T|}\): \(B_U\) contributes \(|B_U|\) factors \(a\),
\(B_T\) contributes \(|B_T|\) factors \(b\), and every other coordinate
contributes \(1\).

Thus the mixed chamber section is squeezed between two measurable product
boxes of equal volume.  Monotonicity of Lebesgue measure gives both
inequalities, so its volume is exactly \(a^{|B_U|}b^{|B_T|}\).
\end{proof}
