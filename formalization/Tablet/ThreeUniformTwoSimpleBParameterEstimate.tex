\begin{lemma}
For every $\eta>0$ there is $D_0$ such that, whenever $D\ge D_0$,
$\mathcal H$ is a $3$-uniform $2$-simple hypergraph with maximum degree at
most $D$, and $e\in E(\mathcal H)$, one has
\[
b_{L(\mathcal H),3D}(e)\le 1-\frac29+\frac{2}{3^5}+\eta .
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $\eta>0$. By \noderef{ThreeUniformTwoSimpleEventualNumericalError},
choose $D_{\rm err}$ such that its displayed numerical inequality holds
for every $D\ge D_{\rm err}$. By \noderef{ExtremalBParameterSaturation},
choose $D_{\rm sat}$ such that its conclusion holds for every
$D\ge D_{\rm sat}$. Set $D_0=\max(1,D_{\rm err},D_{\rm sat})$ and fix
$D\ge D_0$ and an admissible pair $(\mathcal H,e)$.

Apply \noderef{ThreeUniformTwoSimpleFiniteExtremalAttainment} at this
positive $D$. It supplies a finite-vertex, finite-edge admissible pair
$(\mathcal F,f)$ whose local $b$-value dominates that of every admissible
pair, in particular $(\mathcal H,e)$. Its universal maximality is exactly
the premise of \noderef{ExtremalBParameterSaturation}, which gives degree
$D$ at every vertex of $f$ and exactly $3(D-1)$ neighboring edge indices.

Apply \noderef{ThreeUniformTwoSimpleSaturatedMatrixCertificate} to this
pair. It constructs an injective enumeration of $f$ and the sorted big
columns, padded by actual dummy slots when necessary. Write $A$ for its
normalized matrix, with $n\ge3$ columns, and put
\[
Q=\sum_j\sum_{i<i'}A_{ij}A_{i'j},\qquad
P=\operatorname{per}(A_{[3],[3]}),\qquad
L=\sum_{i,j\le3}A_{ij},\qquad r=1+18D^{-1/3}.
\]
The certificate gives entries in $[0,1]$, decreasing column sums bounded
by one, and total entry sum at most three. Therefore
\noderef{ThreeByNMatrixBound} gives $Q/9+P/27-L/27\le2/3^5$.
Also $Q\ge0$ since its summands are products of nonnegative entries,
and $L\le3$ by adding the first three column bounds. Finally $r\ge1$.
Thus \noderef{ThreeUniformTwoSimpleMatrixRescaling} gives
\[
\frac{r^2Q}{9}+\frac{r^3P}{27}-\frac{rL}{27}
\le r^3\frac2{3^5}+\frac{(r^2-1)r}{9}.
\]
In particular, the correction for the negative linear term is retained;
the different homogeneities are not replaced by a single scale factor.

The saturated certificate also supplies the consistently loosened bound
\[
b_{L(\mathcal F),3D}(f)\le
1-\frac29+\frac29D^{-1/3}+\frac8{9D}
+\frac{r^2Q}{9}+\frac{r^3P}{27}-\frac{rL}{27}.
\]
Substitute the preceding rescaling inequality. Since $D\ge D_{\rm err}$,
\noderef{ThreeUniformTwoSimpleEventualNumericalError} bounds all the
terms after $1-2/9$ by $2/3^5+\eta$. This proves the desired estimate
for $(\mathcal F,f)$. The extremal comparison then proves it for the
original $(\mathcal H,e)$.
\end{proof}
