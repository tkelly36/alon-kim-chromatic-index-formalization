\begin{helper}
Let $H\in H(3,2,D)$ and let $v$ have degree less than $D$.
The hypergraph obtained by adding two fresh vertices and one edge consisting
of those vertices and $v$, as in \noderef{KUniformFreshEdgeHypergraph},
belongs to $H(3,2,D)$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The intersection bound two implies the bound three, so $H\in H(3,3,D)$.
Apply \noderef{KUniformFreshEdgePreservesClass} with $k=3$ to obtain
uniformity three and maximum degree at most $D$ for the enlarged hypergraph.
It remains to check the stronger intersection bound two. For distinct old
edge indices, the intersection is exactly the injective image of the old
intersection, so its cardinality is at most two. The intersection of the
new edge with any old edge is contained in $\{v\}$, since the other two
vertices are fresh, so its cardinality is at most one. There is only one
new edge index, so no pair of distinct new indices occurs. These cases
prove 2-simplicity and hence the asserted class membership.
\end{proof}
