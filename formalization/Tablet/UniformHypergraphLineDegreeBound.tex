\begin{lemma}
If $H$ is a finite $k$-uniform hypergraph with maximum degree at most the
natural number $n$, then every edge $e$ has degree at most $k(n-1)$ in
$L(H)$, where subtraction in $n-1$ is natural subtraction.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use \noderef{LineGraphOfHypergraph} and \noderef{HypergraphDegree}.
For each $v\in e$, the set of edges incident with $v$, with $e$ removed,
has size $\deg_H(v)-1\le n-1$. Every neighbor of $e$ belongs to at
least one of these sets. The size of their union is at most the sum of
their sizes, which is at most $|e|(n-1)=k(n-1)$ by
\noderef{UniformHypergraph} and \noderef{MaxDegreeAtMost}.
\end{proof}
