\begin{helper}
Let \(V\) be finite, fix \(v\in V\), and let \(B\subseteq V\setminus\{v\}\).
Then the Lebesgue measure of the chamber
\[
\{q\in\mathbb R^V: 0\le q(x)\le 1\text{ for all }x\in V,\ 
q(z)<q(v)\text{ for all }z\in B\}
\]
is
\[
\int_0^1 a^{|B|}\,da .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Use the measure-preserving coordinate splitting from
\noderef{SamplingOneCoordinateComplementMeasurePreserving}, writing a point of
\(\mathbb R^V\) as \((a,r)\), where \(a=q(v)\) and
\(r\in\mathbb R^{V\setminus\{v\}}\).  Under this splitting the chamber becomes
the set of pairs with \(a\in[0,1]\), all remaining coordinates \(r(w)\in[0,1]\),
and \(r(z)<a\) for every \(z\in B\).

For each fixed \(a\in[0,1]\), the fiber in the remaining coordinates has
Lebesgue measure \(a^{|B|}\) by
\noderef{SamplingOneCoordinateChamberFiberVolume}.  For \(a\notin[0,1]\), the
fiber is empty.  Tonelli's theorem for product Lebesgue measure therefore
identifies the chamber measure with the integral over \(a\in[0,1]\) of
\(a^{|B|}\), which is the displayed formula.
\end{proof}
