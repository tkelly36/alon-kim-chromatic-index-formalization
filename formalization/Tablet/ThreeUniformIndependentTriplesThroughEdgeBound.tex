\begin{lemma}
Let $H$ be a hypergraph with finitely many edge indices, let $f$ be an
edge whose vertices are covered by $v_0,v_1,v_2$, and suppose each
$v_i$ has degree $D$. Let $T$ be a finite family of three-element sets
of edge indices meeting $f$, with distinct members of each set
nonadjacent in the line graph. If $g$ contains $v_i$, then at most
$D^2$ members of $T$ contain $g$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{ThreeUniformIndependentTripleRootLabeling} to each
member $S$ of $T$ containing $g$. Its root-labelled tuple $q_S$ has
image $S$, and its $i$th entry is $g$ by the uniqueness clause.
For each of the other two indices $j$, its entry belongs to the
degree fiber of $v_j$, which has cardinality $D$ by
\noderef{HypergraphDegree}. The tuples therefore lie in a product of
one singleton and two sets of size $D$, of cardinality $D^2$.
The assignment is injective because the image of each tuple recovers
$S$. The asserted bound follows.
\end{proof}
