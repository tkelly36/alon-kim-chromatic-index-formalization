\begin{helper}
Let \(0<\gamma\), let \(S\) and \(T\) be two finite blocker classes, and put
\(p=\gamma/\Delta\).  On the ordered priority chamber \(0\le b\le a\le1\),
the affine change of variables
\[
x=\gamma(1-a),\qquad y=\gamma(1-b)
\]
sends the chamber to \(0\le x\le y\le\gamma\) and gives
\[
\int_0^1\int_0^a
p^2 a^{|S|}b^{|T|}\,db\,da
=
\int_0^\gamma\int_x^\gamma
\frac1{\gamma^2}p^2
\left(1-\frac{x}{\gamma}\right)^{|S|}
\left(1-\frac{y}{\gamma}\right)^{|T|}\,dy\,dx .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The substitution \(b=1-y/\gamma\) carries the interval \(0\le b\le a\) to
\(\gamma\ge y\ge \gamma(1-a)\), with differential \(dy=\gamma\,db\).  Thus,
for each fixed \(a\),
\[
\int_{\gamma(1-a)}^\gamma
\left(1-\frac{y}{\gamma}\right)^{|T|}\,dy
=
\gamma\int_0^a b^{|T|}\,db .
\]
Next substitute \(a=1-x/\gamma\).  The interval \(0\le a\le1\) becomes
\(\gamma\ge x\ge0\), again with differential \(dx=\gamma\,da\).  Applying the
two substitutions successively gives
\[
\int_0^\gamma\int_x^\gamma
p^2\left(1-\frac{x}{\gamma}\right)^{|S|}
\left(1-\frac{y}{\gamma}\right)^{|T|}\,dy\,dx
=
\gamma^2\int_0^1\int_0^a p^2a^{|S|}b^{|T|}\,db\,da .
\]
Dividing by \(\gamma^2\), which is legitimate because \(0<\gamma\), proves the
displayed identity.  This is exactly the Jacobian factor for passing from the
two actual priority coordinates on an ordered chamber to the paper's
\((x,y)\)-coordinates.
\end{proof}
