\begin{lemma}
For positive natural numbers $k,n$, a finite $k$-uniform hypergraph of
maximum degree at most $n$ admits a proper edge-coloring with $kn$ colors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{UniformHypergraphLineDegreeBound}, every vertex of the line
graph has degree at most $k(n-1)<kn$.
Apply \noderef{FiniteGraphGreedyColoring} with $q=kn$.
Adjacency in \noderef{LineGraphOfHypergraph} means distinct intersecting
edges, so this coloring satisfies \noderef{ProperEdgeColoring} and hence
\noderef{ChromaticIndexAtMost}.
\end{proof}
