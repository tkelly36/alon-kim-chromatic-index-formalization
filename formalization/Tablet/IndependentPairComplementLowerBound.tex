\begin{helper}
Let $G$ be a finite simple graph, let $v$ be a vertex, and let
$N=N_G(v)$.  Let $A$ be the set of two-element subsets of $N$ whose two
vertices are adjacent in $G$.  If $|A|\le B$, then
\[
\binom{|N|}{2}-B\le P_G(v).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IndependentPairCount}, $P_G(v)$ is the number of two-element
subsets $S\subseteq N_G(v)$ such that no two distinct vertices of $S$ are
adjacent in $G$.  The set of all two-element subsets of $N$ has cardinality
$\binom{|N|}{2}$.  It is partitioned into the independent two-subsets counted
by $P_G(v)$ and the adjacent two-subsets in $A$: for a two-element set, failure
of the condition that every distinct pair in the set is nonadjacent is exactly
the existence of two distinct vertices of the set that are adjacent.  Therefore
\[
\binom{|N|}{2}=P_G(v)+|A|.
\]
Since $|A|\le B$, subtracting at most $B$ adjacent pairs leaves at most the
number of independent pairs, so
\[
\binom{|N|}{2}-B\le P_G(v).
\]
\end{proof}
