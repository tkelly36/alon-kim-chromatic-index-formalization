\begin{lemma}
For every $\eta>0$, for all sufficiently large integers $D$, uniformly
over all setups $S$ in \noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleLocalSetup} and
all $0\le\delta\le0.031$ with $P=(1+\delta)D^2-9D$, one has
\[
T_2\le(1/9+1.33\delta+\eta)D^3.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take $D\ge100$. The assumed pair equality implies
$P\le1.031D^2-9D$. Apply \noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleSmallXs}
with this cutoff and the given nonnegative $\delta$ to obtain
$WX_s\le(2/75)\delta D^2$.
By \noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimplePairLowerBound},
$P\ge D^2-9D+Y$, whence $Y\le\delta D^2$.
The setup gives $Y\ge0$, $W=WX_s+WX_b$, and
$P=3D^2-6D+3-W+Y$. Substitution yields
\[
WX_b=(2-\delta)D^2+3D+3+Y-WX_s
 \ge (2-77\delta/75)D^2.
\]
Also \noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleBigPartSize} gives $|X_b|=6$.
Set $\delta'=77\delta/75\ge0$ and
$\varepsilon_0=\sqrt{8\delta'/3}$. Applying
\noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleBalancedBigDegree} with $\delta'$
therefore gives $|\sigma(g)-(4/3)D|\le\varepsilon_0D$ for every $g\in V_2$.
We have $\varepsilon_0^2\le (8/3)(77/75)(31/1000)<1/4$, hence
$0\le\varepsilon_0\le1/2$.

Set $\varepsilon=\max\{\varepsilon_0,D^{-1/4}\}$. This is strictly
positive, including when $\delta=0$, and at most $1/2$ for $D\ge100$.
The cross-degree bound remains true with $\varepsilon$ in place of
$\varepsilon_0$, since $D>0$. Moreover
$\varepsilon^2\le\varepsilon_0^2+D^{-1/2}$.
All hypotheses of \noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleT2TriangleBound}
are now verified, so
\[
T_2\le\frac1{12}\left(
 \frac{(8/3)(P+WX_s)+(4+2\varepsilon)Y}{D^2}
 -\frac43+3\varepsilon^2\right)D^3+\frac43D^2.
\]
Here $(4+2\varepsilon)Y\le5\delta D^2$, since $Y\ge0$,
$\varepsilon\le1/2$, and $Y\le\delta D^2$.
Also $3\varepsilon^2\le8(77/75)\delta+3D^{-1/2}$.
Insert these inequalities, the bound on $WX_s$, and the pair equality.
All multipliers are nonnegative because $D>0$. Dropping the negative
contribution from $-9D$ in the pair equality gives
\[
T_2\le\left(\frac19+\frac{3589}{2700}\delta\right)D^3
 +\frac14D^{5/2}+\frac43D^2
 \le(1/9+1.33\delta)D^3+\frac{19}{12}D^{5/2}.
\]
The coefficient computation is
$((8/3)(77/75)+5+8(77/75))/12=3589/2700<133/100$;
the final inequality uses $\delta\ge0$ and $D^2\le D^{5/2}$ for $D\ge1$.
Choose an integer $D_0\ge100$ with
$D_0\ge(19/(12\eta))^2$. For every $D\ge D_0$, positivity gives
$19/(12\sqrt D)\le\eta$, so the error term is at most $\eta D^3$.
This threshold depends only on $\eta$, not on the setup or $\delta$,
and proves the stated uniform estimate.
\end{proof}
