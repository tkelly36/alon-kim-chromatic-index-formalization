\begin{lemma}
Let $s$ be a subset of a finite linearly ordered set $V$, and let
$f:V\to R$ take values in a commutative semiring. Then
\[
\sum_{\substack{p\subseteq s\\|p|=2}}\prod_{v\in p}f(v)
=\sum_{v\in s}\sum_{\substack{w\in s\\v<w}}f(v)f(w).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Map each ordered pair $(v,w)\in s\times s$ with $v<w$ to $\{v,w\}$.
Its image is a two-element subset of $s$. Every two-element subset has
exactly one increasing enumeration, so this map is surjective. If two
increasing pairs have the same underlying set, their smaller elements
agree, and then their larger elements agree, proving injectivity.
The product over $\{v,w\}$ is $f(v)f(w)$ because $v\ne w$.
Reindexing the finite sum along this bijection and then summing first over
$v$ and next over the allowed $w$ gives the identity.
\end{proof}
