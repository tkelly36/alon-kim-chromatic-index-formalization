\begin{helper}
For every finite simple graph $G$ and vertex $v$, the number of independent
pairs in the neighborhood of $v$ is at most
\[
\binom{|N_G(v)|}{2}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IndependentPairCount}, an independent pair counted at $v$ is a
two-element subset $S$ of the neighbor set $N_G(v)$ satisfying the additional
condition that no two distinct vertices of $S$ are adjacent in $G$.  Forgetting
this additional nonadjacency condition gives an injection from the counted
family into the family of all two-element subsets of $N_G(v)$.  The latter
family has cardinality $\binom{|N_G(v)|}{2}$, so the independent-pair count is
bounded by that binomial coefficient.
\end{proof}
