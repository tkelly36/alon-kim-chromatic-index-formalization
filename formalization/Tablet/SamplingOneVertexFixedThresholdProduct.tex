\begin{helper}
Fix a finite simple graph $G$, a vertex $v$, a regularity value $\Delta$ with
$\deg_G(v)=\Delta$ for every vertex $v$, a sampling parameter $\gamma$, and a
priority threshold $a$.  For each activation-decision function
$b:V(G)\to\{\mathrm{inactive},\mathrm{active}\}$, give an activated copy of
$v$ weight $\gamma/\Delta$, an inactive copy of $v$ weight $0$, an activated
neighbor of $v$ weight $(\gamma/\Delta)a$, an inactive neighbor of $v$ weight
$1-\gamma/\Delta$, an activated non-neighbor different from $v$ weight
$\gamma/\Delta$, and an inactive non-neighbor different from $v$ weight
$1-\gamma/\Delta$.  Then the total activation-decision weight is
\[
\sum_b\prod_{u\in V(G)}W_u(b(u))
=
\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a)\right)^\Delta .
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{SamplingOneVertexActivationDecisionProduct} with
\[
p=\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
q=1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}.
\]
It gives
\[
\sum_b\prod_{u\in V(G)}W_u(b(u))
=
\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\prod_{u\in V(G)}
\begin{cases}
1, & u=v,\\
1-\gamma/\Delta+(\gamma/\Delta)a, & u\sim_G v,\\
1, & u\ne v\text{ and }u\not\sim_G v,
\end{cases}
\]
because the non-neighbor factor is
$(1-\gamma/\Delta)+\gamma/\Delta=1$.

For a neighbor $u$ of $v$, the remaining nontrivial factor satisfies
\[
1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}+\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}a
=
1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a).
\]
The factor at $u=v$ is $1$, and a simple graph has no loop at $v$, so this
special vertex does not contribute to the neighbor count.  Every
non-neighbor also contributes the factor $1$.  Therefore the whole product is
the constant
$1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a)$ raised to the number of neighbors of $v$.
By the regularity hypothesis, this number is $\deg_G(v)=\Delta$.  Substituting
this into the preceding displayed formula gives exactly
\[
\sum_b\prod_{u\in V(G)}W_u(b(u))
=
\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a)\right)^\Delta .
\]
\end{proof}
