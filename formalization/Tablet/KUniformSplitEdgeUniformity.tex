\begin{helper}
Let $\mathcal F$ be a $k$-uniform hypergraph, let $g$ be an edge of
$\mathcal F$, let $v\in g$, and let $x_1$ be one of the $k-1$ fresh
vertices in the split-edge construction of
\noderef{KUniformSplitEdgeHypergraph}.  Then
$\operatorname{SplitEdge}_k(\mathcal F,g,v,x_1)$ is $k$-uniform.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{UniformHypergraph}, we must check that every edge in the
split hypergraph has cardinality $k$.  The construction is described in
\noderef{KUniformSplitEdgeHypergraph}.  Every old edge label other than
$g$ is unchanged except that its vertices are included into the enlarged
vertex set, so its cardinality remains $k$.

For the old label $g$, the split construction replaces $g$ by
$(g\setminus\{v\})\cup\{x_1\}$.  Since $v\in g$ and $g$ originally has
$k$ vertices, $g\setminus\{v\}$ has $k-1$ vertices.  The vertex $x_1$ is
fresh, so it is disjoint from the old vertices in $g\setminus\{v\}$.
Thus the modified old edge has $(k-1)+1=k$ vertices.

The one new edge is $\{v\}$ together with all $k-1$ fresh vertices.  The
old vertex $v$ is disjoint from the fresh vertices, so this new edge has
$1+(k-1)=k$ vertices.  Hence every edge in the split hypergraph has
cardinality $k$, proving $k$-uniformity.
\end{proof}
