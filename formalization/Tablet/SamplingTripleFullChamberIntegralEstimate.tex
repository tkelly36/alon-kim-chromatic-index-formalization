\begin{helper}
Let $\Delta>0$, let $0\le\gamma\le\Delta$, and let
$x,y,z>0$ and $\tau\ge0$.  Put $M=x+y+z+\tau$.  For the three unordered
pairs in a fixed independent triple, suppose the paired ordered chambers have
middle exponents $N_x,N_y,N_z$ and lower exponents
$L_x,L_y,L_z$ satisfying
\[
\frac{N_x}{\Delta}=x,\quad \frac{L_x}{\Delta}=\rho_x,\quad
\frac{N_y}{\Delta}=y,\quad \frac{L_y}{\Delta}=\rho_y,\quad
\frac{N_z}{\Delta}=z,\quad \frac{L_z}{\Delta}=\rho_z,
\]
where $\rho_x,\rho_y,\rho_z\ge0$, and assume
\[
1+x+\rho_x=1+y+\rho_y=1+z+\rho_z=M .
\]
Then the three paired finite full-graph chamber integrals obtained after
\noderef{SamplingTripleActivationChamberSum} satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
&2\int_{0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma}
\left(1-\frac w\Delta\right)^\Delta
\left(1-\frac v\Delta\right)^{N_x}
\left(1-\frac u\Delta\right)^{L_x}\,dw\,dv\,du \\
&\quad+
2\int_{0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma}
\left(1-\frac w\Delta\right)^\Delta
\left(1-\frac v\Delta\right)^{N_y}
\left(1-\frac u\Delta\right)^{L_y}\,dw\,dv\,du \\
&\quad+
2\int_{0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma}
\left(1-\frac w\Delta\right)^\Delta
\left(1-\frac v\Delta\right)^{N_z}
\left(1-\frac u\Delta\right)^{L_z}\,dw\,dv\,du \\
&\le
\frac{2}{(1+x)M}+\frac{2}{(1+y)M}+\frac{2}{(1+z)M}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
We prove the estimate for the first paired term; the other two are identical
with $(x,\rho_x,N_x,L_x)$ replaced by $(y,\rho_y,N_y,L_y)$ or
$(z,\rho_z,N_z,L_z)$.

On the finite chamber $0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma$, the hypothesis
$\gamma\le\Delta$ gives $0\le u/\Delta,v/\Delta,w/\Delta\le1$.  Hence every
base $1-u/\Delta$, $1-v/\Delta$, and $1-w/\Delta$ is nonnegative.  The
standard inequality $1-t\le e^{-t}$ for $0\le t\le1$, applied term by term
and then raised to the corresponding natural exponents, gives
\[
\left(1-\frac w\Delta\right)^\Delta
\left(1-\frac v\Delta\right)^{N_x}
\left(1-\frac u\Delta\right)^{L_x}
\le
e^{-w}\,e^{-xv}\,e^{-\rho_x u}.
\]
Here the exponent identities use
$N_x/\Delta=x$ and $L_x/\Delta=\rho_x$.

The exponential kernel is nonnegative.  Therefore enlarging the region from
the finite chamber $0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma$ to the infinite chamber
$0\le u\le v\le w<\infty$ can only increase the integral.  Thus the first
paired finite chamber contribution is at most
\[
2\int_0^\infty\int_u^\infty\int_v^\infty
e^{-w}e^{-xv}e^{-\rho_xu}\,dw\,dv\,du .
\]
The three elementary integrations are legitimate because
$1>0$, $x>0$, and $\rho_x\ge0$.  First
\[
\int_v^\infty e^{-w}\,dw=e^{-v}.
\]
Then
\[
\int_u^\infty e^{-(1+x)v}\,dv
=\frac{e^{-(1+x)u}}{1+x},
\]
since $1+x>0$.  Finally
\[
\int_0^\infty \frac{e^{-(1+x+\rho_x)u}}{1+x}\,du
=\frac{1}{(1+x)(1+x+\rho_x)}.
\]
Multiplying by the factor $2$ and using $1+x+\rho_x=M$ gives the bound
\[
2\int_{0\le u\le v\le w\le\gamma}
\left(1-\frac w\Delta\right)^\Delta
\left(1-\frac v\Delta\right)^{N_x}
\left(1-\frac u\Delta\right)^{L_x}\,dw\,dv\,du
\le \frac{2}{(1+x)M}.
\]

Applying the same argument to the other two paired chamber terms gives
$2/((1+y)M)$ and $2/((1+z)M)$.  Adding the three inequalities proves the
displayed estimate.  The final comparison of these three rational terms with
the square-sum expression in the paper is deliberately left to
\noderef{SamplingTripleRealChamberEstimate}.
\end{proof}
