\begin{helper}
Let $\Delta>0$, let $L\ge0$, and fix a natural exponent $N$ and a parameter
$\alpha$.  Suppose that on the window $0\le x\le y\le L$ the two finite
survival factors satisfy the pointwise lower bounds
\[
\left(1-\frac{x}{\Delta}\right)^N
\ge (1-\alpha)e^{-\alpha L}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\left(1-\frac{y}{\Delta}\right)^\Delta
\ge (1-\alpha)e^{-y}.
\]
Assume also that \(1-\alpha\ge0\) and that the exponential window has the
margin
\[
\frac53+2\alpha
\le
2\int_0^L\int_x^L
(1-\alpha)e^{-\alpha L}(1-\alpha)e^{-y}\,dy\,dx .
\]
Then the finite high-overlap window contribution satisfies
\[
\frac2{\Delta^2}\int_0^L\int_x^L
\left(1-\frac{x}{\Delta}\right)^N
\left(1-\frac{y}{\Delta}\right)^\Delta\,dy\,dx
\ge
\frac{5/3+2\alpha}{\Delta^2}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The proof is the direct finite-window comparison used in the high-overlap
case.  Since \(1-\alpha\ge0\) and exponentials are positive, the lower bound
\((1-\alpha)e^{-\alpha L}\) for the first factor is nonnegative.  On every
point of the chamber \(0\le x\le y\le L\), the two assumed pointwise
inequalities and multiplication by nonnegative quantities give
\[
\left(1-\frac{x}{\Delta}\right)^N
\left(1-\frac{y}{\Delta}\right)^\Delta
\ge
(1-\alpha)e^{-\alpha L}(1-\alpha)e^{-y}.
\]
Both sides are continuous on the compact window, so interval-integral
monotonicity first in \(y\in[x,L]\), and then in \(x\in[0,L]\), gives
\[
\int_0^L\int_x^L
\left(1-\frac{x}{\Delta}\right)^N
\left(1-\frac{y}{\Delta}\right)^\Delta\,dy\,dx
\ge
\int_0^L\int_x^L
(1-\alpha)e^{-\alpha L}(1-\alpha)e^{-y}\,dy\,dx .
\]
Multiplying by the positive scale factor \(2/\Delta^2\) preserves the
inequality.  The assumed exponential-window margin then yields
\[
\frac2{\Delta^2}\int_0^L\int_x^L
\left(1-\frac{x}{\Delta}\right)^N
\left(1-\frac{y}{\Delta}\right)^\Delta\,dy\,dx
\ge
\frac{5/3+2\alpha}{\Delta^2},
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
