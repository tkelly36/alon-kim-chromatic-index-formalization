\begin{helper}
Let $I$ be a finite nonempty linearly ordered index set, and let
$(u_a)_{a\in I}$ be a real family.  Then
\[
\sum_{\substack{a<b\\a,b\in I}}u_a u_b
\le
\frac{|I|-1}{2|I|}\left(\sum_{a\in I}u_a\right)^2.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Put $S=\sum_{a\in I}u_a$ and $Q=\sum_{a\in I}u_a^2$.  Expanding $S^2$
over ordered pairs gives
\[
S^2=\sum_{a,b\in I}u_a u_b.
\]
The diagonal ordered pairs contribute $Q$.  The off-diagonal ordered pairs
split into the two strict orders $a<b$ and $b<a$, and the swap
$(a,b)\mapsto (b,a)$ is a bijection between those two parts.  Therefore
\[
S^2=Q+2\sum_{a<b}u_a u_b. \tag{1}
\]
By the finite Cauchy--Schwarz inequality,
\[
S^2\le |I|\,Q,
\]
so, since $|I|>0$, we have $Q\ge S^2/|I|$.  Substituting this lower bound
for $Q$ in (1) yields
\[
2\sum_{a<b}u_a u_b
=S^2-Q
\le S^2-\frac{S^2}{|I|}
=\frac{|I|-1}{|I|}S^2.
\]
Dividing by $2$ gives the claimed inequality.
\end{proof}
