\begin{helper}
Let $G$ be a finite graph with activation--priority measure $\nu$, and fix an
activation atom $A$.  For vertices $a,b,c$, put $Q=\{a,b,c\}$.  Let
$C_{pqr}$ denote the event that $A$ is the activation set, that
\[
\pi(p)\le \pi(q)\le \pi(r),
\]
and that every vertex of $\{p,q,r\}$ beats every activated neighbor in $A$.
Then
\[
\nu\{A\text{ is the activation set and every }q\in Q\text{ beats every
activated neighbor in }A\}
\le
\sum_{(p,q,r)}
\nu(C_{pqr}),
\]
where the sum is over the six ordered triples
\[
(a,b,c),\ (a,c,b),\ (b,a,c),\ (b,c,a),\ (c,a,b),\ (c,b,a).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let $E$ be the fixed-activation survival event on the left-hand side.  For any
point of $E$, the three real priority coordinates $\pi(a),\pi(b),\pi(c)$ are
linearly ordered.  Hence at least one of the six weak orders
\[
\pi(a)\le\pi(b)\le\pi(c),\quad
\pi(a)\le\pi(c)\le\pi(b),\quad
\pi(b)\le\pi(a)\le\pi(c),
\]
\[
\pi(b)\le\pi(c)\le\pi(a),\quad
\pi(c)\le\pi(a)\le\pi(b),\quad
\pi(c)\le\pi(b)\le\pi(a)
\]
holds.  Ties cause no difficulty because the chamber inequalities are weak.
Since each ordered triple is a permutation of $a,b,c$, the set
$\{p,q,r\}$ appearing in the chamber event is the same set $Q$.  Therefore
every point of $E$ lies in the union of the six chamber events
$C_{abc},C_{acb},C_{bac},C_{bca},C_{cab},C_{cba}$.

Applying finite subadditivity of the measure $\nu$ to this six-set cover gives
the displayed inequality.
\end{proof}
