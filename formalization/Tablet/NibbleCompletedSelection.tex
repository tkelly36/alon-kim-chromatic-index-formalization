\begin{definition}
For a completion datum $Q$ and an outcome
$\omega\in(\mathbb R^2)^{\{(a,v):v\in\operatorname{Fin}(n_a)\}}$,
the completed selection $J_a(\omega)$ consists of those vertices $v$ of
$Q_a$ whose activation coordinate is $1$ and whose priority is strictly
greater than the priority of every activated neighbor in $Q_a$.
\end{definition}
