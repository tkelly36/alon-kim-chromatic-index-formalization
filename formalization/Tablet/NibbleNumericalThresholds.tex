\begin{lemma}
For integers $A,k\ge1$ and $\iota>0$, there exists an integer
$\gamma_0\ge1$ such that, for every integer $\gamma\ge\gamma_0$, there
exists $D_0\ge1$ such that for all integers $D\ge D_0$, writing
$\Delta=kD$, $\ell=\lceil\Delta/\gamma\rceil$, and
$q=4A(k+1)k^4D^6$, we have $\gamma\le\Delta$, $\ell>0$, and
\[
\left(1-\frac{1-e^{-\gamma}}\Delta+\frac2{\Delta^2}\right)^\ell
\le1-\frac{1-\iota/2}\gamma,
\]
\[
e^{-\iota^2D/(2\gamma^2 A)}\le e^{-(\log D)^2},\qquad
e^{-\iota^2\ell/(8+2\iota)}\le e^{-(\log D)^2},\qquad
e\,e^{-(\log D)^2}(q+1)\le1.
\]
The thresholds depend only on the displayed parameters.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose $\gamma_0\ge1$ so large that $e^{-\gamma_0}\le\min(1/4,\iota/8)$
and $1/\gamma_0\le\iota/4$. Fix $\gamma\ge\gamma_0$.
Choose $D$ large enough that $\Delta\ge\max(\gamma,4,16/\iota)$.
Put $u=1-e^{-\gamma}-2/\Delta$. Then $0\le u\le1$, and
$e^{-\gamma}+2/\Delta+u^2/\gamma\le\iota/2$.
Since $0\le1-u/\Delta\le1$ and $\ell\ge\Delta/\gamma$,
\[
(1-u/\Delta)^\ell\le e^{-u\ell/\Delta}
\le e^{-u/\gamma}\le1-u/\gamma+u^2/\gamma^2
\le1-(1-\iota/2)/\gamma.
\]
The exponential inequality for the nonpositive argument $-u/\gamma$
follows, for example, by integrating the second derivative of $e^x$ on
the interval between that argument and zero, where $e^x\le1$.
Positivity of $\Delta/\gamma$ gives $\ell>0$.

The constants $c_1=\iota^2/(2\gamma^2A)$ and
$c_2=\iota^2k/((8+2\iota)\gamma)$ are positive. For any $c>0$,
$cD\ge(\log D)^2$ eventually: putting $s=\log D$, the exponential series
gives $e^s\ge s^3/6$ for $s>0$, hence $s^2/e^s\le6/s\to0$.
Apply this to $c_1,c_2$ and use $\ell\ge kD/\gamma$ for the two tails.
Finally put $C=4A(k+1)k^4$. For $D\ge1$,
$CD^6+1\le(C+1)D^6$, and the logarithm of the required local-lemma
expression is at most $1+\log(C+1)+6\log D-(\log D)^2$, which is
nonpositive eventually. Choose an integer $D_0$ above these finitely many
thresholds. None uses the hypergraph, its lists, or $b$.
\end{proof}
