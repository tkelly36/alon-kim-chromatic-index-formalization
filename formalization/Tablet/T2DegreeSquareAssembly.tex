\begin{lemma}
Let $A$ be a finite index type, let $q,c,y:A\to\mathbb R$, and let
$D,\varepsilon,m,B,Y$ be real numbers. Suppose $D\ge0$,
$0<\varepsilon\le1/2$, and, for every $i\in A$,
\[
0\le q_i\le2D,\quad q_i\le2D-c_i+y_i,\quad y_i\ge0,
\quad |c_i-4D/3|\le\varepsilon D.
\]
If $\sum_i y_i\le2Y$ and $\sum_i c_i\ge2B-Dm$, then
\[
\sum_i q_i^2\le(20/9+\varepsilon^2)|A|D^2
-\tfrac83 DB+\tfrac43 mD^2+(16/3+2\varepsilon)DY.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $d_i=2D-c_i$. The cross-degree assumption gives
$(2/3-\varepsilon)D\le d_i\le(2/3+\varepsilon)D$; in particular $d_i\ge0$.
Multiplying $q_i\le d_i+y_i$ first by $q_i\ge0$ and then by
$d_i\ge0$ gives
$q_i^2\le d_i^2+d_i y_i+y_iq_i$.
Thus $q_i^2\le d_i^2+((2/3+\varepsilon)D+2D)y_i$.
Reindexing $A$ by $\{0,\ldots,|A|-1\}$ and applying
\noderef{BoundedSquaresInterval} yields
\[
\sum_i d_i^2\le\tfrac43D\sum_i d_i
-|A|(4/9-\varepsilon^2)D^2.
\]
Also $\sum_i d_i=2D|A|-\sum_i c_i\le2D|A|-2B+Dm$.
Sum the pointwise square estimate, substitute these two inequalities,
and use $\sum_i y_i\le2Y$. Both multiplying coefficients,
$4D/3$ and $(8/3+\varepsilon)D$, are nonnegative. Expansion gives
exactly the stated inequality.
\end{proof}
