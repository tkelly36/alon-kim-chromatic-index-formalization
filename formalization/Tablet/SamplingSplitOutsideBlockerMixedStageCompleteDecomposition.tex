\begin{helper}
For one side of a split-blocker mixed stage, a complete-stage decomposition
is obtained from the actual two-piece set decomposition and the normalized
mass representatives; it is not an additional hypothesis.  Let
\(S_k(\omega)\) be a complete stage on the cell \(C_\omega\), let
\(U_k(\omega)\) be the unchanged part, and let \(B_k^\partial(\omega)\) be the
current-boundary part.  Let \(H(k,\omega)\), \(U(k,\omega)\), and
\(B^\partial(k,\omega)\) be their normalized real representatives with common
cell weight \(w(\omega)\).

Assume that, for each \(k<n\) and \(\omega\), the weight and representatives
are nonnegative, and that all three representatives are \(0\) when
\(w(\omega)=0\).  Assume also that
\[
S_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega
 =
(U_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega)\cup(B_k^\partial(\omega)\cap C_\omega),
\]
that the two pieces on the right are disjoint, and that finite additivity of
the measure on this displayed two-piece cover gives
\[
\nu(S_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega)
 =
\nu(U_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega)
 +\nu(B_k^\partial(\omega)\cap C_\omega).
\]
Finally assume the three normalized mass identities
\[
\operatorname{ofReal}(w(\omega)H(k,\omega))
 = \nu(S_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega),
\]
\[
\operatorname{ofReal}(w(\omega)U(k,\omega))
 = \nu(U_k(\omega)\cap C_\omega),
\qquad
\operatorname{ofReal}(w(\omega)B^\partial(k,\omega))
 = \nu(B_k^\partial(\omega)\cap C_\omega).
\]
Then
\[
H(k,\omega)=U(k,\omega)+B^\partial(k,\omega)
\]
for every \(k<n\) and every \(\omega\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(k<n\) and \(\omega\).  We prove the displayed real identity by cases on
the common weight.

If \(w(\omega)=0\), the zero-weight convention gives
\[
H(k,\omega)=0,\qquad U(k,\omega)=0,\qquad
B^\partial(k,\omega)=0.
\]
Therefore \(H(k,\omega)=U(k,\omega)+B^\partial(k,\omega)\).

Now suppose \(w(\omega)\ne0\).  Since \(w(\omega)\ge0\), we have
\(w(\omega)>0\).  By finite additivity and the three normalized mass
identities,
\[
\operatorname{ofReal}(wH)
 =
\operatorname{ofReal}(wU)+\operatorname{ofReal}(wB^\partial),
\]
where the arguments are evaluated at the fixed \(k,\omega\).  The products
\(wH\), \(wU\), and \(wB^\partial\) are nonnegative because the common weight
and the three representatives are nonnegative.  Hence, after applying
\(\operatorname{toReal}\) to the preceding extended-nonnegative-real equality,
\[
wH=wU+wB^\partial=w(U+B^\partial).
\]
Cancelling the positive factor \(w(\omega)\) gives
\[
H(k,\omega)=U(k,\omega)+B^\partial(k,\omega).
\]
Since \(k\), \(k<n\), and \(\omega\) were arbitrary, the conclusion follows
for all stages and atoms.
\end{proof}
