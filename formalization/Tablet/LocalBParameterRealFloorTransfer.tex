\begin{lemma}
Let $H$ be a finite $k$-uniform hypergraph, where $k\ge1$.
If $r\ge1$ is real and all vertex degrees of $H$ are at most $r$, then,
for $n=\lfloor r\rfloor_+$ and every edge $e$,
\[
b^{\mathbb R}_{L(H),kr}(e)\le b_{L(H),kn}(e).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{RealDegreeFloorBound}, the maximum degree is at most $n$.
We have $n\ge1$, and \noderef{UniformHypergraphLineDegreeBound} bounds
the line degree of $e$ by $k(n-1)\le kn$. Thus $0<kn\le kr$ are
admissible scales. Apply \noderef{LocalBParameterRealAntitone} to these
scales and then \noderef{LocalBParameterRealNatCast} at $kn$.
\end{proof}
