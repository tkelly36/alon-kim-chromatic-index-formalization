\begin{helper}
Let $\mathcal F$ be a finite multi-hypergraph, let $f$ be an edge, and let
$v$ be a vertex.  In the fresh-edge hypergraph
$\operatorname{FreshEdge}_k(\mathcal F,v)$ of
\noderef{KUniformFreshEdgeHypergraph}, the number of independent pairs in the
line-graph neighborhood of the old-edge copy $\operatorname{inl}(f)$ is at most
the number of independent pairs in the line-graph neighborhood of $f$ in
$\mathcal F$ plus the number of old line-graph neighbors of $f$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IndependentPairCount}, such a pair is a two-element subset of the
line-graph neighborhood whose two members are not adjacent to each other.  By
\noderef{LineGraphOfHypergraph}, line-graph adjacency is exactly nonempty
intersection of the underlying hyperedges, together with distinctness of edge
labels.  By \noderef{KUniformFreshEdgeHypergraph}, every old edge is copied by
the old-edge inclusion, and the only non-old edge label is the single fresh
edge.

Partition the independent pairs counted around $\operatorname{inl}(f)$ in the
fresh-edge hypergraph into two classes.  The first class consists of pairs not
containing the fresh edge.  Both members are then old-edge copies.  The
fresh-edge construction preserves intersections among old-edge copies exactly,
so this first class injects into the independent pairs counted around $f$ in
the original line graph: send each pair of old copies to the corresponding pair
of old labels.

The second class consists of pairs containing the fresh edge.  Since the pair
has exactly two members and there is only one fresh edge label, its other
member is an old-edge copy.  Because the pair is counted in the neighborhood of
$\operatorname{inl}(f)$, that old-edge copy is a line-graph neighbor of
$\operatorname{inl}(f)$ in the fresh hypergraph.  Again old-edge intersections
are preserved by \noderef{KUniformFreshEdgeHypergraph}, so the corresponding
old label is a line-graph neighbor of $f$ in $L(\mathcal F)$.  Thus the second
class injects into the old neighbor set of $f$.

The two classes are disjoint and exhaust the fresh-hypergraph independent-pair
count.  Adding the two injection bounds gives
\[
  P_{L(\operatorname{FreshEdge}_k(\mathcal F,v))}(\operatorname{inl}(f))
  \le P_{L(\mathcal F)}(f)+\deg_{L(\mathcal F)}(f),
\]
which is the asserted count-level upper bound.
\end{proof}
