\begin{helper}
Fix a finite simple graph $G$, a vertex $v$, a regularity value $\Delta$ with
every vertex having degree $\Delta$, and a sampling parameter $\gamma$.  Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{A\subseteq V(G)}{\bf 1}_{v\in A}
\left(\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}\right)^{|A|}
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}\right)^{|V(G)|-|A|}
\int_0^1 a^{|\{u:u\in A,\ u\sim_G v\}|}\,da  \\
&\qquad =
\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\int_0^1
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a)\right)^\Delta\,da .
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed priority threshold $a$, identify an activation set $A$ with
its Boolean activation-decision function $b:V(G)\to\{\mathrm{inactive},
\mathrm{active}\}$.  The summand on the left is zero unless $v$ is active.
When $v\in A$, its Bernoulli atom contributes one factor $\gamma/\Delta$ for
$v$, each activated neighbor of $v$ contributes an additional factor $a$, each
other activated vertex contributes $\gamma/\Delta$, and each inactive vertex
different from $v$ contributes $1-\gamma/\Delta$.  Thus the finite sum over
activation sets at threshold $a$ is exactly the activation-decision weight
computed in \noderef{SamplingOneVertexFixedThresholdProduct}.

That node gives the pointwise identity
\[
\sum_A{\bf 1}_{v\in A}
\left(\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}\right)^{|A|}
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}\right)^{|V(G)|-|A|}
a^{|\{u:u\in A,\ u\sim_G v\}|}
=
\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}
\left(1-\frac{\gamma}{\Delta}(1-a)\right)^\Delta .
\]
All sums are finite and all integrands are polynomials in $a$, hence
integrable on $[0,1]$.  Integrating this pointwise identity over $a\in[0,1]$
and moving the finite sum through the integral gives the displayed formula.
\end{proof}
