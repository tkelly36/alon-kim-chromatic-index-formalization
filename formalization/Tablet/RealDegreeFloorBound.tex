\begin{lemma}
If every vertex degree of a finite hypergraph $H$ is at most a real number
$r$, then $H$ has maximum degree at most $\lfloor r\rfloor_+$, the
nonnegative natural floor of $r$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each degree in \noderef{HypergraphDegree} is a natural number. A natural
number at most $r$ is at most its natural floor. Applying this at every
vertex gives \noderef{MaxDegreeAtMost}.
\end{proof}
