\begin{helper}
Let $\nu$ be a measure on activation-priority pairs, let $Q$ be a fixed
finite set of vertices, and suppose that the activation atom of every
$A\subseteq V$ has measure
\[
\nu\{\omega:\omega_1=A\}=\operatorname{ofReal}(\operatorname{atom}(A)).
\]
Assume $\operatorname{atom}(A)\ge 0$ for every $A$ and that $F(A)\ge 0$ whenever
$Q\subseteq A$.  Then
\[
\sum_A {\bf 1}_{Q\subseteq A}\,
\nu\{\omega:\omega_1=A\}\operatorname{ofReal}(F(A))
=
\operatorname{ofReal}\left(
\sum_A {\bf 1}_{Q\subseteq A}\,\operatorname{atom}(A)F(A)\right).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The sum is finite, since the vertex set is finite and the activation sets are
finite subsets of it.  For each activation set $A$ with $Q\subseteq A$, replace
the measure of the atom $\{\omega:\omega_1=A\}$ by
$\operatorname{ofReal}(\operatorname{atom}(A))$ using the assumed atom law.
For activation sets not containing $Q$, both summands are zero by the displayed
indicator.

For a retained activation set, $\operatorname{atom}(A)\ge 0$, so
$\operatorname{ofReal}(\operatorname{atom}(A)F(A))$ equals
$\operatorname{ofReal}(\operatorname{atom}(A))\operatorname{ofReal}(F(A))$.
Moreover, on the retained terms $F(A)\ge 0$, and hence
$\operatorname{atom}(A)F(A)\ge 0$.  On discarded terms the real summand is
zero.  Therefore every real summand inside the final finite sum is
nonnegative, and the standard finite-sum rule for $\operatorname{ofReal}$ over
nonnegative summands converts the finite sum of extended nonnegative real
terms into the single $\operatorname{ofReal}$ of the corresponding real finite
sum.
\end{proof}
