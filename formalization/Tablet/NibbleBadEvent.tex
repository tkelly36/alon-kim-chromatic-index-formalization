\begin{definition}
Let $W=\bigcup_{e\in E}H(e)$, a finite set even when the vertex type is
infinite. For a completion datum $Q$ and real thresholds $d,z$, the bad
events are indexed by the disjoint union $W\sqcup E$:
$B_{\mathrm{inl}(x)}=\{Y_x>d\}$ and
$B_{\mathrm{inr}(e)}=\{|\operatorname{Del}_e|\ge z\}$, using the selections
of the common product experiment.
\end{definition}
