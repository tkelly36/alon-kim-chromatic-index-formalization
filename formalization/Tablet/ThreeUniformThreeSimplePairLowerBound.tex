\begin{lemma}
In a $3$-uniform $3$-simple local extremal setup at scale $D$, let
$\mathcal F\in H(3,3,D)$, $f$, $X$, $P(f)$, $w(X)$, and
$Y=Y_{\mathcal F}(f)$ be as in
\noderef{ThreeUniformThreeSimpleLocalSetup}.  Then
\[
P(f)\ge D^2-9D+Y_{\mathcal F}(f).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $X=X(f,\mathcal F)$, and write
\[
w(X)=\sum_{x\in X}\sum_{v<v'\in f}
\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)\deg_{\mathcal F}(v',x).
\]
By \noderef{SaturatedLineGraphEdgeCount}, applied to the saturated edge
$f$, the line-graph edge count inside $N_{L(\mathcal F)}(f)$ is
$3\binom{D-1}{2}+w(X)-Y_{\mathcal F}(f)$ with
$Y_{\mathcal F}(f)\ge0$.  Since the neighborhood has $3D-3$ vertices, the
independent pairs are the nonedges in that induced line graph, so
\[
P(f)=\binom{3D-3}{2}-3\binom{D-1}{2}-w(X)+Y_{\mathcal F}(f)
=3D^2-6D+3-w(X)+Y_{\mathcal F}(f).
\]

The table with rows indexed by the three vertices of $f$ and columns indexed
by $X$ has entries $a_{v,x}=\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)$.  The saturated structure
\noderef{ThreeUniformSaturatedEdgeStructure} gives the column bound: for every
$x$, the column sum is at most $D$.  It also gives row sums $2(D-1)$, hence
total table sum $6(D-1)$.
Applying \noderef{TablePairBound} with $k=3$, $C=D$, and
$T=6(D-1)$ gives
\[
w(X)\le \frac13\cdot 6(D-1)D\le 2D^2 .
\]
Substituting this into the identity for $P(f)$ yields
\[
P(f)\ge 3D^2-6D+3-2D^2+Y_{\mathcal F}(f)
=D^2-6D+3+Y_{\mathcal F}(f)
\ge D^2-9D+Y_{\mathcal F}(f),
\]
for $D$ in the sufficiently large range of the proof.  This is the desired
bound.
\end{proof}
