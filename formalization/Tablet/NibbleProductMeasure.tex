\begin{definition}
For a finite coordinate type $T$ and real $p$, the nibble product measure
on $(\mathbb R\times\mathbb R)^T$, with its Borel product sigma algebra,
is $\bigotimes_{t\in T}\kappa_p$, where
\[
\kappa_p=(\operatorname{ofReal}(p)\delta_1+
\operatorname{ofReal}(1-p)\delta_0)\otimes
 (\operatorname{Leb}|_{[0,1]}).
\]
The first coordinate is the activation indicator and the second is the
priority. Here $\operatorname{ofReal}$ truncates negative numbers to zero.
For $0\le p\le1$ this is the independent Bernoulli--uniform experiment.
\end{definition}
