\begin{helper}
Let $\mathcal F$ be a $1$-uniform hypergraph.  For any two edge labels
$f$ and $g$, if $g\cap f$ is nonempty, then $|g\cap f|=1$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{UniformHypergraph}, the edge $f$ has exactly one vertex.
The intersection $g\cap f$ is a subset of $f$, so it has at most one
vertex.  By hypothesis the intersection is nonempty, so it has at least
one vertex.  Therefore its cardinality is exactly one.
\end{proof}
