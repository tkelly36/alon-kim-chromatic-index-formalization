\begin{lemma}
Let $D$ be sufficiently large, let $\mathcal F$ be a $3$-uniform
$2$-simple hypergraph of maximum degree at most $D$, and let
$f\in E(\mathcal F)$ be an edge with $f=\{v_1,v_2,v_3\}$.  Assume that
every vertex of $f$ has degree $D$, that $f$ intersects exactly $3(D-1)$
other edges of $\mathcal F$, and that the following saturated-edge
conclusions hold for $f$: every edge other than $f$ that meets $f$ meets it
in exactly one vertex, for every $x\in X(f,\mathcal F)$ one has
$\sum_{v\in f}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)\le D$, and for every $v\in f$ one has
$\sum_{x\in X(f,\mathcal F)}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)=2(D-1)$.

Let $X=X(f,\mathcal F)$, and define
\[
X_s=\left\{x\in X:\sum_{v\in f}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)<D^{2/3}\right\},
\qquad
X_b=X\setminus X_s .
\]
For every $v\in f$,
\[
\sum_{x\in X_b}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)\le D+18D^{2/3}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The hypotheses place the edge $f$ in the situation covered by
\noderef{SaturatedEdgeStructure}; in particular, for every $u\in f$ we have
\[
\sum_{x\in X}\deg_{\mathcal F}(u,x)=2(D-1),
\]
and every $x\in X$ satisfies
$\sum_{u\in f}\deg_{\mathcal F}(u,x)\le D$.  Since each vertex of $X_b$ has
total degree into $f$ at least $D^{2/3}$, summing over $X_b$ gives
\[
|X_b|D^{2/3}
\le \sum_{x\in X_b}\sum_{u\in f}\deg_{\mathcal F}(u,x)
\le \sum_{x\in X}\sum_{u\in f}\deg_{\mathcal F}(u,x)
=6(D-1).
\]
Thus $|X_b|\le 6D^{1/3}$.

Fix $v\in f$.  Because $\mathcal F$ is $2$-simple, for any two distinct
vertices $x,x'\in X_b$ there is at most one edge containing the triple
$\{v,x,x'\}$: two such edges would share the two vertices $v$ and $x$ (and
also $v$ and $x'$).  Hence the number of edges through $v$ that contain two
vertices of $X_b$ is at most $\binom{|X_b|}{2}\le 18D^{2/3}$, for all
sufficiently large $D$.

There are exactly $D-1$ edges through $v$ other than $f$, because $v$ has
degree $D$ and one of those edges is $f$.  At most $18D^{2/3}$ of them
contain two vertices of $X_b$, so at least $D-1-18D^{2/3}$ of them contain a
vertex of $X_s$.  Each neighboring edge through $v$ contains two vertices
outside $f$, and \noderef{SaturatedEdgeStructure} gives the total outside
incidence through $v$ as $2(D-1)$.  Therefore
\[
\sum_{x\in X_b}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)
=2(D-1)-\sum_{x\in X_s}\deg_{\mathcal F}(v,x)
\le 2(D-1)-(D-1-18D^{2/3})
\le D+18D^{2/3}.
\]
\end{proof}
